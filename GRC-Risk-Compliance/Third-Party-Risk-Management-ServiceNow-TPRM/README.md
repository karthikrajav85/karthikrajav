# Third-Party Risk Management Assessment Using ServiceNow TPRM

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a practical Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) assessment performed using ServiceNow Vendor Risk Management (VRM/TPRM) as part of a simulated vendor onboarding process within a financial services environment.

The assessment was conducted for FinSecure Digital Bank during the onboarding of PayFlow Technologies, a payment processing service provider supporting digital banking operations. Since the vendor would potentially handle business-critical services and customer-related information, a structured third-party risk assessment was required before onboarding approval could be granted.

Using ServiceNow TPRM, I performed key activities typically carried out by Technology Risk, Cyber Risk, and Compliance teams. These activities included initiating a due diligence request, managing vendor onboarding workflows, conducting inherent risk assessments, reviewing questionnaire responses, evaluating vendor-provided information, coordinating assessment activities through the third-party portal, identifying a compliance-related issue, and managing issue remediation through closure.

The project provided hands-on exposure to vendor risk management processes commonly used in regulated industries such as banking, financial services, insurance, and technology organizations. It also helped develop practical experience with risk assessment workflows, due diligence activities, questionnaire management, issue management, vendor communication, and risk-based decision making.

This portfolio project demonstrates how third-party risks can be identified, assessed, tracked, and addressed through a structured governance process using the ServiceNow TPRM platform.

---

## Business Scenario

FinSecure Digital Bank is a rapidly growing digital banking organization that provides online banking, mobile banking, payment processing, and merchant services to its customers. As part of its business expansion strategy, the bank planned to onboard PayFlow Technologies as a third-party payment processing service provider.

Because payment processors handle sensitive financial transactions and may have access to customer-related information, FinSecure's risk management team required a formal Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) assessment before approving the vendor onboarding process. The objective was to evaluate whether the vendor's security, compliance, privacy, and operational practices aligned with the organization's risk management requirements.

The assessment was performed using ServiceNow Vendor Risk Management (VRM/TPRM), which provides a structured workflow for vendor onboarding, due diligence, risk assessments, questionnaire management, issue tracking, and remediation activities.

During the assessment process, the vendor was required to provide information regarding its security controls, compliance practices, privacy requirements, and risk management processes. The responses were reviewed to identify potential risks that could impact FinSecure's business operations, regulatory obligations, or customer trust.

The project simulated a real-world third-party risk assessment process commonly performed by Technology Risk, Cyber Risk, Compliance, Vendor Risk Management, and Governance teams within banking and financial services organizations.

## Assessment Methodology and Execution Approach

The assessment followed a structured Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) methodology using ServiceNow Vendor Risk Management (VRM/TPRM). The objective was to evaluate the vendor's risk posture, identify potential compliance concerns, and determine whether the vendor could be onboarded within the organization's risk tolerance.

The assessment was performed through the following phases:

### Phase 1 – Due Diligence Request and Vendor Onboarding

- Initiated the Due Diligence Request (DDR) process.
- Reviewed vendor onboarding requirements.
- Established the vendor relationship within ServiceNow.
- Defined the business engagement and service scope.

### Phase 2 – Internal Risk Assessment (IRQ)

- Conducted an Internal Risk Questionnaire (IRQ).
- Evaluated inherent risk factors associated with the vendor.
- Assessed business criticality and service impact.
- Reviewed assessment results and calculated risk scores.

### Phase 3 – Due Diligence Assessment

- Initiated the due diligence phase.
- Created a third-party assessment package.
- Assigned assessment activities to the vendor.
- Prepared security, privacy, and business continuity questionnaires.

### Phase 4 – Vendor Assessment Review

- Reviewed vendor-submitted questionnaire responses.
- Evaluated responses against expected compliance and risk requirements.
- Assessed security, privacy, and operational control information.
- Identified areas requiring additional clarification.

### Phase 5 – Issue Management and Remediation

- Identified a compliance-related concern during questionnaire review.
- Created an issue record within ServiceNow TPRM.
- Submitted the issue to the vendor for clarification and remediation.
- Reviewed the vendor's response and supporting information.
- Validated corrective actions and documented findings.

### Phase 6 – Assessment Closure

- Performed final assessment review.
- Verified issue resolution activities.
- Closed the issue workflow.
- Completed the third-party assessment lifecycle.

This methodology reflects a typical Third-Party Risk Management process used by Technology Risk, Vendor Risk Management, Cyber Risk, and Governance teams to evaluate vendors before onboarding and to ensure identified risks are appropriately managed and documented.

## Key Findings and Risk Assessment Results

The assessment evaluated the vendor's responses across multiple risk domains, including cybersecurity, privacy, business continuity, and compliance. The objective was to identify potential risks that could impact FinSecure Digital Bank before onboarding the vendor into a business-critical payment processing role.

### Inherent Risk Assessment Results

The Internal Risk Questionnaire (IRQ) was used to evaluate the inherent risk associated with the vendor relationship before considering any mitigating controls.

Key risk factors reviewed included:

- Business criticality of the service provided.
- Potential impact on customer-facing operations.
- Dependency on the vendor for payment processing activities.
- Exposure to sensitive business and customer information.
- Operational impact in the event of vendor disruption.

The assessment established a baseline understanding of the risks associated with onboarding the vendor and provided the foundation for the due diligence process.

---

### Security Assessment Observations

The vendor completed the NIST-based assessment questionnaire, which was designed to evaluate the organization's security governance and cybersecurity practices.

Areas reviewed included:

- Security control implementation.
- Risk management practices.
- Information security governance.
- Security program maturity.
- Security-related responsibilities and oversight.

Based on the responses received, no critical security deficiencies requiring immediate escalation were identified during the assessment.

---

### Business Continuity Assessment Observations

The Business Continuity Management (BCM) questionnaire was reviewed to assess the vendor's preparedness for operational disruptions.

Areas reviewed included:

- Business continuity planning.
- Recovery preparedness.
- Service resilience.
- Continuity management practices.

The responses indicated that the vendor maintained business continuity processes sufficient for the scope of the assessment and no major continuity concerns were identified.

---

### Privacy and Compliance Assessment Finding

The most significant finding identified during the assessment originated from the CCPA privacy questionnaire.

#### Observation

During the questionnaire review, the vendor indicated that supporting documentation for CCPA compliance was not available.

#### Risk Consideration

The absence of documented privacy compliance evidence could indicate:

- Inadequate compliance governance.
- Weak privacy control documentation.
- Increased regulatory and compliance risk.
- Potential challenges during future audits or compliance reviews.

Because the vendor was supporting a business-critical payment processing function, the response required further investigation before assessment closure.

---

### Issue Management Actions

To validate the observation, an issue record was created within ServiceNow TPRM and submitted to the vendor for clarification.

Actions performed:

- Documented the compliance concern.
- Created a formal issue record.
- Requested clarification from the vendor.
- Reviewed the vendor response.
- Performed remediation validation.

The issue was tracked through the complete issue management lifecycle, including submission, review, remediation, validation, and closure.

---

### Remediation Outcome

During the remediation process, the vendor clarified that the questionnaire response had been entered incorrectly and confirmed that supporting documentation for CCPA compliance was available.

Following review of the vendor's explanation:

- The concern was reassessed.
- The remediation response was validated.
- The issue was determined to be resolved.
- The issue record was formally closed.

---

Based on the assessment activities performed, questionnaire reviews conducted, and remediation actions completed, the identified compliance concern was successfully addressed through the ServiceNow TPRM workflow.

Final assessment outcome:
