# Third-Party Risk Management Assessment Using ServiceNow TPRM

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a complete Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) assessment performed using ServiceNow Vendor Risk Management (VRM).

The assessment was conducted for FinSecure Digital Bank during the onboarding of a payment processing vendor.

---

## Organization

FinSecure Digital Bank

## Third Party

PayFlow Technologies

## Service

Digital Banking Payment Processing Service

## Vendor Criticality

Critical Vendor

---

## Business Objective

Evaluate the cybersecurity, compliance, and operational risks associated with onboarding PayFlow Technologies and determine whether the vendor can be accepted within FinSecure's risk appetite.

---

## Activities Performed

### Vendor Onboarding
- Created third-party vendor profile
- Created engagement record
- Defined vendor service relationship

### Inherent Risk Assessment
- Reviewed vendor service criticality
- Evaluated potential business impact
- Determined initial risk exposure

### Due Diligence Assessment
- Reviewed vendor questionnaire responses
- Evaluated compliance-related evidence
- Identified response gaps

### Issue Management
- Created issue for missing CCPA compliance evidence
- Submitted issue to vendor for clarification
- Tracked remediation activities

### Vendor Remediation
- Vendor acknowledged incorrect questionnaire response
- Vendor confirmed supporting compliance documentation exists
- Response reviewed and validated

### Risk Evaluation
- Assessed residual risk
- Determined issue resolution status
- Finalized assessment

---

## Key Finding

The vendor initially responded that no documentation existed to support CCPA compliance.

A risk issue was created and submitted to the vendor for clarification.

The vendor later confirmed the response was entered incorrectly and stated that supporting documentation does exist.

After review and validation, the issue was closed.
